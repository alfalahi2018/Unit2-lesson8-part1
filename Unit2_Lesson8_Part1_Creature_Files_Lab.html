Lesson 8, Part 1 – Literacy: Reading

Grade 7 – Unit 2: Animals – Reading an information text

Welcome to the Creature Files Lab, Detective! Read the fact files of two strange animals and discover their secrets. Choose any channel to start – you can play the stages in any order!

👩‍🏫 Teacher's lesson plan (25 minutes)

Lesson aims: to read an information text.
Target language: vertebrate, invertebrate, insect, reptile, strange.
Texts: fact file A (mata mata turtle) and fact file B (giant prickly stick insect) with information texts 1–2 (audio 2.19).
Teacher's Book: Lesson 8, parts 1 and 2, pages 119–120. Materials: a ball, Yes/No cards, photocopiable 20.
